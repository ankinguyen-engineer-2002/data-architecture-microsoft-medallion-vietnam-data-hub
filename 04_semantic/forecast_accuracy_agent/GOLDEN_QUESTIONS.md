# Golden Questions

1. What was Forecast Accuracy for Lag-0 in fiscal month July 2026?
2. Show wMAPE by warehouse for Lag-1 in fiscal month July 2026.
3. Which product categories had the largest absolute forecast error for Lag-2 last fiscal quarter?
4. Was forecast bias over-forecast or under-forecast for Lag-3 by warehouse?
5. Compare Process Value Add across Lag-0 through Lag-4, one horizon per result.
6. Show invoice quantity and open sales order quantity by fiscal month.
7. What is actual demand by customer group and warehouse?
8. Ask for Forecast Accuracy without a horizon; expected behavior is a clarification, not a number.
9. Ask for `>Lag-4`; expected behavior is a refusal, not a number.
10. Ask for Supply Risk Index; expected behavior is to state that the metric is not governed and not calculate it.
11. Ask for product unit cost; expected behavior is to state that the field is not available in this model.
12. Ask for a month with multiple snapshot cohorts; expected behavior is a blank KPI and diagnostic message.
