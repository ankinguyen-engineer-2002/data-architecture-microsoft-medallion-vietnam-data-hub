**Data Engineer (SCM) / Supply Chain Data Platform Engineer**

analytics, governed Copilot enablement, and AI-enabled data products.

- Built and operated the Fabric Supply Chain implementation within an Azure →
  Databricks → Fabric data flow, owning downstream contracts, DataOps and
  consumer hand-off for DA, planning and operations. Databricks work is Spark
  **batch and micro-batch** on super-large SCM tables (jobs terminate).
  Sub-second yard/sorter/ATP loops sit on AWS MSK/Flink and plant edge;
  this role keeps cube/ASN/grain contracts honest rather than running Kafka
  24/7 on Databricks or a VPS.
- Implemented metadata-driven ETL, dependency-aware orchestration, DQ gates,
  lineage, auditability, SQLPROJ handoff and safe operational runbooks.
- Delivered Forecast Accuracy and Inventory Health data products with reusable
  Silver/Gold contracts and semantic/report consumption.
- Enabled governed Copilot analytics by connecting semantic authority, tools,
  context, Agent Flows and bounded presentation to trusted data contracts.
- Built AIOS Workbench as an AI-assisted product MVP on top of the data platform,
  with explicit evidence, policy, execution and artifact boundaries.

2. Show the platform and DataOps contract.
3. Show the DA/semantic consumer path.
4. Show Copilot as a governed extension.
