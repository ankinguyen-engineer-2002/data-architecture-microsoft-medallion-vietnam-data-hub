## Context

Bronze source DQ audit found 11/33 tables FAIL. US team (Rakesh/DE) provided feedback for 8 tables. This plan covers the first 6 tables with combined analysis from DQ audit + US feedback + live verification. Tables 7-11 will be covered in a follow-up plan.

Key finding: Most Bronze "duplicates" are caused by **grain mismatch** between DQ audit grain (curated/business) and source PK grain (AS400/EDW). Silver/Gold layers already have guardrails for most tables. Only 1-2 tables need actual code change.
